Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical project timeline?

+

Timeline varies based on project scope. Simple logos typically take 3-5 days, websites 2-4 weeks, and illustrations depend on complexity. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you offer revisions?

+

Absolutely! I include unlimited revisions until you're completely satisfied. You maintain total creative control throughout the co-creation process.

What's your payment structure?

+

50% deposit is required to begin the project, and the remaining 50% is due upon final approval. This protects both of us and ensures mutual commitment.

Do you provide post-launch support?

+

Yes, I offer 30 days of free post-delivery support for websites and digital projects. Extended support packages are available for ongoing needs.

Can you work with my existing brand guidelines?

+

Of course! Whether you have existing guidelines or need me to create them, I'll ensure consistency across all your materials.

How can I get started?

+

Simply fill out the contact form above or send me an email. I'll get back to you within 24 hours to schedule your free initial consultation.
